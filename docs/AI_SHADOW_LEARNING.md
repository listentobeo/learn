# Beo assignment-review shadow learning

## Purpose

The future review assistant will first learn in shadow mode while Benjamin continues every real assignment review manually. It will not publish feedback, mark reviews complete, or replace Benjamin during this learning period.

## What the system learns from

For each reviewed assignment, retain an authorised, versioned learning record containing:

- Track, lesson code, week number, lesson notes, assignment instructions, and the three quiz concepts.
- The submitted image and Discovery written response where applicable and consented.
- Benjamin's original written feedback.
- The strengths Benjamin repeatedly notices.
- The priority correction Benjamin chooses.
- The language and tone Benjamin uses for different ages and skill levels.
- The focus of the one-to-one call.
- The final reviewed/completed outcome.

These are the repeated signals that describe Benjamin's review method. Student identity should be removed from model-learning inputs unless it is strictly required and explicitly consented to.

## Shadow stages

### Stage 1 — structured manual signals

Benjamin reviews normally. The admin form quietly structures his final notes into rubric scores, positive callout, priority fix, and call focus. No AI suggestion is shown.

### Stage 2 — private shadow draft

Before seeing Benjamin's final review, the model creates a private draft from the lesson rubric and submission. After Benjamin completes his manual review, the system compares both versions and records agreements, missed details, unsafe assumptions, and tone differences.

### Stage 3 — assisted review

After enough consistently accurate comparisons, Benjamin may see the AI draft as an editable starting point. Students and parents still see only Benjamin-approved feedback.

### Stage 4 — supervised automation

Only after measured quality thresholds are maintained can routine first-pass reviews be automated. Benjamin remains able to edit, override, or disable the assistant and remains responsible for final school feedback.

## Promotion requirements

Promotion between stages requires:

- A minimum approved-example count across every track and lesson type.
- High agreement with Benjamin on the priority correction and positive callout.
- No penalty for techniques not yet taught.
- Safe performance across ages, skin tones, subjects, materials, camera quality, and device conditions.
- Documented student/parent consent, retention, deletion, and training-use rules.
- Versioned rubrics, prompts, models, costs, failures, edits, and approvals.

The system must never train on student work by default or silently change from shadow mode to student-facing automation.
