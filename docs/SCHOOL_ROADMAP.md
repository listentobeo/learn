# Beo School of Art platform roadmap

## Delivered in the current foundation

- Track-based enrollment, weekly lesson drip, quizzes with retakes, and private assignment uploads.
- Benjamin can open an assignment, which marks it as seen for the student.
- Written feedback is stored per assignment and remains readable by the student or parent.
- WhatsApp review-call request link after submission.
- One configurable welcome video per track, separate from lessons and without a quiz or assignment.
- Accurate admin metrics and student records based on Supabase data.

## Next content phase — after the workbook arrives

- Replace seed content with the final lesson notes, questions, correct answers, and explanations.
- After each attempt, show which answers were correct and teach the right answer for missed questions.
- Let students retake the quiz or continue after reviewing the corrections.
- Keep attempt history while using the latest attempt for current progress reporting.

## School automation phase

- Email and WhatsApp reminders for weekly lesson unlocks, assignments due, missing submissions, and review calls.
- Submission-seen and feedback-ready notifications.
- A structured review-call scheduler; the current WhatsApp link is the lightweight first version.
- Parent-readable progress summaries and feedback history.
- Subscription renewal reminders and self-service card-management links. Recurring payment status handling is already in the platform foundation.

## Completion and progression

- Reusable Beo certificate design generated only when a student completes the entire track.
- Unique certificate verification number and public verification page.
- Locked mentorship path that becomes available only to eligible graduates or invited students.

## Future assisted review

- AI-assisted assignment review only after enough Benjamin-approved examples exist.
- Benjamin remains the final reviewer; suggestions are never sent automatically without review.
- Training data, student consent, retention, and privacy rules must be defined before this phase.
